About Garfield

Designing between the logic of a system and the lived experience of a person.

I’m a multidisciplinary designer with a Product-Service System Design background from Politecnico di Milano. I currently support social projects and service design at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University.

My practice moves between research, strategy, objects, interfaces, visual systems, and physical environments. I also serve as a PSSD Global Circles ambassador for Hong Kong.
